## Core dysfunction

**Inability to distinguish between authentic, systemic communication that shapes and synchronizes environment ("fish rap"), and superficial, performative noise that merely fills space ("bird song").**

Team/organization confuses loudness and visibility with influence, while overlooking subtle, vibrational, invisible forms of communication that truly shape culture, processes, and relationships. The dysfunction lies in orientation toward "what is heard" instead of "what resonates and changes environment."

#### 📘 Fairy tale

# ABOUT THE FISH 
## who rapped

They say fish are silent.
 That they have no voice.
 That they just swim, look, and occasionally get caught.
But that's a mistake. Nonsense.
A dry land lie.

 Because fish don't stay silent.
 Fish rap.

 Not as you imagine – no microphones, no beats from speakers.
 
 "Fish rap is different.
 It's the rhythm of currents.
 It's the low, the high of waves and ripples.
 It's the tempo of water circles.
 It's the space between bubbles.

 Underwater, sound spreads differently.
 Deeper. Wider. Slower.
 And so fish don't roar.
 Fish vibrate.

Every fish species has its own musical style.
Orcas sing chansons – long, nostalgic, influencing current directions.
Sharks sing gospel – deep and with echo. Mixing colorful surface shades with tones of depths.
Whale dares opera 
and jellyfish do vocals for her or take up triangles, bells, chimes.
Dolphin is folklore. 
Octopus cuts jazz improvisations. 
Pufferfish manages brass bands, but doesn't like them.
Piranhas ballads.
And carp tries throat singing. 
Mussels and shells sing love stories.
Flying fish do ska.
Penguin sees himself in classics.
Pop is experted by bleak.
And eel has no musical ambitions.

There's more.
But none of this is music for ears,
 This influences various water properties.

 And the fish?
 Fish has rap.
 Not for fame.
 For precision.

 Flow is king and king is fish.
 Beat is made by sea and lakes.
 Riff drips, flow flows.
 Precise words stream like water.
 Her punchline is a fountain.

 Every verse has weight.
 And this fish can do.
 Better than birds.

 Bird song?
 That's screeching.
 Scream, roar, caw, shriek.
 Tempo? Melody? Lyrics?
 Birds can't give.
 Birds can only rudely curse.
 Peep rhymes with peep, otherwise no idea.
 Whatever they say is immediately censored.

 But fish really give.
 They give rhythm to water.
 They give meaning to silence.
 They give beats to currents.

 And one day it happened that fish began rapping even on the surface.
Not for others.
For water.

 And water answered.
 It rippled differently.
 It trembled to the rhythm.

 It created verses like water circles, within which were more circles.

Mist, fallen into fields, copied their flow.
And created circles in grain.

Fish music was heard – not by ear, but by eye, by body.

 And so drop by drop it spreads all around,
 that first place in the hit parade,
 was won by fish – undoubtedly.

 Not because they sing.
 But because they know..
..when to be silent.

Maybe it's time,
for the world to learn about fish rap.

Maybe it's time,
for
listening to stop and perceiving to begin.

Maybe it's time,
for silence to finally get above the surface.

## 🎯 3 diagnostic questions

1. **Q1 (Interpretation):** "Fish doesn't roar, but 'vibrates'. Her rap is not for show, but for 'precision' and influences the water itself. Why do people/teams prefer 'bird screech' – loud but empty expression – over 'fish rap', which is silent but transforms environment?"

2. **Q2 (Identification):** "Where in your team/organization do you observe 'bird song' – that is, loud, repetitive, but low-content communication (meetings, presentations, reports) that 'only rudely curses' instead of creating meaning? And where on the contrary does 'fish rap' occur – quiet, vibrational work that truly moves things forward, but remains unseen below the surface?"

3. **Q3 (Transformation):** "What would have to happen for your team to begin 'perceiving, not just listening'? How could we cultivate environment where 'silence finally gets a word above the surface' and where we would appreciate 'flow' of precision and influence on system more than loudness of expression?"

## 🧠 Sample angles (3 of 12)

### 1. ANGLE OF VIBRATIONAL COMMUNICATION (Not roar, but resonance)

**How it manifests in team:**
Fish "vibrate". Their communication is bodily, through environment. It doesn't just transmit data, but changes context ("water rippled differently"). Real influence is always vibrational, not declaratory. In team this means we solve what is "said," not what is "felt". We ignore nonverbal communication, tension in room, energy between people.

**What to do about it (sample solution):**
Introduce **"reading vibrations"** – before beginning agenda solving, spend 2 minutes describing "how the air/water feels here today". What is unspoken, but present? Train sensitivity to environment.

---

### 2. ANGLE OF SYSTEMIC IMPACT (Influencing environment)

**How it manifests in team:**
Fish rap "influences various water properties". Real communication is not about passing information, but about changing the system in which we all exist. Quality meeting changes culture, not just informs. When meetings are understood as "necessary evil" for passing information, output is "minutes," not "change in vibration."

**What to do about it (sample solution):**
Measure success of communication not by length or number of points, but by answer to question: **"How did our shared environment (trust, clarity, energy) change after this exchange?"**

---

### 3. ANGLE OF PRECISION BEFORE LOUDNESS (Flow is king)

**How it manifests in team:**
Fish raps "for precision". In her world, value has not the one who is loudest, but the one who is most precise – her words "have weight". In team, those who speak most and most confidently often prevail, while those with precise but quiet observations are overlooked. Discussion becomes competition in rhetoric, not search for precision.

**What to do about it (sample solution):**
Introduce rule of **"weight of word"** – everyone has limited number of "golden words" at meeting. When they use them, they must be silent. Forces economy and precision. Appreciate quality, not quantity of speech.

## 🛠️ Workshop (structure)

### Workshop: "FROM BIRD SCREECH TO FISH RAP" – 90 minutes

**Goal:** Identify "bird" and "fish" patterns of communication in team, strengthen authentic, systemic communication, and cultivate "productive silence."

**Part 1: Diagnosis (30 min)**
- Introduction, reading the fairy tale
- Individual answers to 3 questions
- Sharing in groups: "What is the biggest 'bird screech' in our team? And where is hidden our most precious 'fish rap'?"

**Part 2: Deep work (40 min)**
- Exercise **"Communication Map: Birds vs. Fish"** – write down concrete examples of loud but empty communication (birds) and quiet but effective communication (fish)
- Exercise **"Vibrational audit"** – analyze last significant meeting from perspective of "vibrations" beneath surface and translate them into systemic needs

**Part 3: Action plan (20 min)**
- Selection of one "bird screech" to eliminate and one "fish rap" to strengthen
- Introduction of **"fish minutes"** (silence for reflection) and **"hit parade below surface"** (appreciating invisible successes)

## 📊 Diagnostic matrix

| Dominant angle | Diagnosis | Intervention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bird screech** | Communication is loud, full of jargon and repetition, but low in content. Meetings long, unproductive. | **"Bird screech detox":** Cancel one regular meeting for a month. Introduce async update. Introduce strict timebox. |
| **Precision before loudness** | Team dominated by extroverts and rhetors, introverts with precise observations silenced. | **"Circle of silence":** For discussions use talking object and mandatory silence pauses. Forces equality and thoughtfulness. |
| **Not perceiving vibrations** | Team communicates only content-wise, deaf to emotions, tension, energy. | **"Vibration thermometer":** At beginning and end of meeting everyone shows by thumb how they feel energy in room. |
| **Lack of genre diversity** | Everyone forced to communicate in same, corporate style. | **"Festival of genres":** For one meeting everyone chooses alternative communication style (only metaphors, only images). |
| **Fear of silence** | Silence is awkward, every pause filled with word. Deep reflection impossible. | **"Fish minute":** After key questions 60 seconds of absolute silence for thoughts. |

## 💡 Why Fish is key?

1. **Epidemic of noise:** In times of information overload, endless meetings, and performative sharing, ability to distinguish signal from noise is critical. Fish gives language and framework for this differentiation.

2. **Theme of authenticity in communication:** Fish teaches how to be authentic in the way we express ourselves – not just what we say, but in what "genre" and with what intention.

3. **Counterpole to superficiality:** While other cards solve content dysfunctions, Fish solves the very form and medium of communication. Shows that the way we speak shapes the environment in which we live.

4. **Offers positive vision:** Fish is not just about diagnosis of dysfunction ("bird screech"), but mainly about vision of functional alternative ("fish rap," "strategic silence," "communication with environment"). It is a card of hope and concrete change.

5. **Seventh basic archetype:** With FISH card, the basic set of seven cards is completed, forming complete diagnostic ecosystem:
   - 🐟 **FISH** – Authentic communication vs. noise
   - ⭐ **STARFISH** – Depth vs. surface
   - 🦩 **FLAMINGO** – Energy vs. direction
   - 🐈‍⬛ **TOMCAT** – Closure vs. openness
   - 🦄 **UNICORN** – Image vs. hybridity
   - 🐉 **DRAGON** – Rules vs. life
   - 🐀 **MOUSE** – Attention vs. overlooking

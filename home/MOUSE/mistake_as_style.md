### 🐘 Big idea about "lesser evil"

## 👩‍🎨 About the author: MISTAKE AS STYLE

*Description of creative process aka: I need to mess something up for it to start being interesting.*

People pretend they create with vision and control. In reality, most valuable things are born at the moment when the plan falls apart and instead of panic, the person becomes curious.

### 🧠 My learning mechanism

1. **Intention**  
   *"I'll paint it like this."*  
   Brain runs on familiar pattern.

2. **Mistake**  
   Something shows up.  
   - color bleeds  
   - shape slips  
   - composition falls apart  
   ➡ Autopilot turned off.

3. **Adapt**  
   *"What now?"*  
   → new solution  
   → different stroke  
   → different path  
   → new style  

And suddenly something alive emerges.  
Not because I planned it.  
Because I responded.  

Only thanks to the mistake does it start to get interesting.

---

### 🤖 As long as everything goes according to plan

- routine can run  
- minimal attention  
- minimal discovery  

**Mistake switches brain to mode:**  
✔ focus  
✔ seeking solutions  
✔ experiment  
✔ presence  

This is the state when we truly learn.

---

### ⚡ Mistake as disruption of autopilot

Brain saves energy. Likes to repeat the known.  
Mistake = pattern disruption  
→ attention on  
→ new connections form  

Without disruption, no growth.

---

### 🕊️ When it's "ruined"

I stop being afraid of messing it up.  
And although no one told me how it should be right, the pressure of perfection disappears.

**Perfection binds. Ruinedness liberates.**

Once *"it's already destroyed"*:  
→ you can experiment  
→ you can risk  
→ you can play  

And play is space for discovery.

---

### 🔥 Historically: mistakes move the world

(Not a metaphor. Fact.)

- **Ceramics** arose from over-fired clay.  
- **Glass** from overheated sand.  
- **Penicillin** from contaminated culture.  
- **Post-it** from failed glue.  

Failure = new category.

---

### 🐑 Animals vs. humans

Sheep reacts by instinct.  
Human proves:  
- remember the mistake  
- change behavior  
- create tool  
- change environment  

---

### 📚 Learning is not memorizing

It is **strategy change**.  
"To learn" is not stuffing knowledge – that's a school shortcut.

Real learning is:  
✔ expanding perspectives  
✔ ability to see connections  
✔ orientation in the unknown  
✔ ability to find information  
✔ adaptation to situation  

Not having everything in head.  
Knowing **how to think**.

---

### ⚠️ Past infallibilities

When the goal is never to make mistakes:  
- you reserve safety procedures  
- you don't risk  
- you don't experiment  
- you don't expand possibilities  

Result: correct, but dead.

---

### 🐘 Big idea about "lesser evil"

- **Big risk:** visible, monitored, regulated, controlled.  
- **Small tiny mistakes:** overlooked, accumulate, normalize, harm long-term.

In systems, catastrophe often doesn't kill.  
Small neglects do.

---

### 🌈 Why things aren't as we expect

Because brain loves simple stories:  
- good × bad  
- safe × dangerous  
- correct × mistaken  

Reality is:  
- context  
- impacts  
- time  
- connections  

**Dictionary of gaps** shows how to see these layers.

---

### 🧩 Summary

Mistake wakes you from automatism.  
Waking turns on attention.  
Attention opens new possibilities.  
And there begins real learning.

And maybe creation will really start to engage me only at the moment when I stop controlling and start responding to the reality that emerges.

This is not about process of failure.  
This is **the process**.

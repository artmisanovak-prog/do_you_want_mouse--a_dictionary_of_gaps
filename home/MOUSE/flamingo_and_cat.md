# 🦩 + 🐈‍⬛ Combination: Flamingo & Tomcat

**Diagnosis:** "Enthusiastically jumping into speech during the fall"

## Description of the combined problem

When the dysfunctions of Flamingo (energy without direction) and Tomcat (jumping into speech) join, a team emerges that:

1. **Falls with enthusiasm** – celebrates activity, even when heading downward
2. **Jumps into each other's speech** – interrupts itself in outbursts of optimism
3. **Doesn't listen to warnings** – "Lalala" drowns out any doubts
4. **Closes quickly** – pushes for decisions in order to "continue flying"

## New diagnostic questions (for this combination)

1. **Q1:** "When did your team last get so enthusiastic about an idea that it stopped noticing where it was actually heading?"
2. **Q2:** "Who in the team most often 'jumps into speech with optimism' and thereby interrupts critical voices?"
3. **Q3:** "What rituals could slow down this 'enthusiastic fall' and create space for reflection?"

## Sample of combined angles

### 1. 🦩🐱 TOXIC JUMPING
- **From Flamingo:** Need to maintain enthusiasm at any cost
- **From Tomcat:** Compulsion to finish others' thoughts
- **Combination:** Interrupting critical remarks with even greater enthusiasm
- **Solution:** "Minute of silence after every enthusiastic thought" – mandatory 60 seconds of reflection

### 2. 🦩🐱 FALL WITH COMMENTARY
- **From Flamingo:** Aesthetization of the fall ("falling with grace")
- **From Tomcat:** Commenting on everything in real time
- **Combination:** During crisis, more energy is devoted to describing the situation than solving it
- **Solution:** "Ban on meta-communication during crisis" – solve the problem, don't talk about it

## Workshop idea for this combination

**"Half hour of silence during the fall"** (90 minutes)

1. **Part 1:** Identification of "enthusiastic falls" in the team (20 min)
2. **Part 2:** Exercise "Slowed dialogue" – speak slowly, listen, don't interrupt (30 min)
3. **Part 3:** Proposal of "emergency brakes" – mechanisms that automatically slow the team down during enthusiasm (40 min)

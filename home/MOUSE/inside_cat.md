# Combination within one card: 🐈‍⬛ × 🐈‍⬛

**Mini-workshop name:** "Dot, or three dots?"

**Goal:** Practice the transition from closing (Angle 1: Tyranny of the dot) to opening (Angle 9: Unfinished as gift).

**Process (15 minutes):**
1. **Closing Phase (5 min):** Team discusses a short problem. Facilitator strictly monitors the rule from Angle 1: "The first one to say 'dot' only summarizes."
2. **Reflection (2 min):** "What was that feeling like? What happened to your ideas?"
3. **Opening Phase (5 min):** On the same problem. Facilitator activates the rule from Angle 9: "Forbid finishing sentences. Always start with the word 'It is like...' and don't finish."
4. **Final reflection (3 min):** "Which approach brought unexpected thoughts? Where in our work is there room for 'three dots'?"

**Generative principle:** Even one card contains a dialectic (problem ↔ solution). By combining its own angles, you create a new, concrete tool.

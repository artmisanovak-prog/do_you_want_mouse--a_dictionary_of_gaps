# ABOUT THE FLAMINGO
## who was upside down

__________

The flamingo sang. 
Lalalá. 
High, 
higher than he should. 
Higher than was safe. 
Higher than was reasonable.

“Let’s fly together!” he shouted. 
“We’ll crash down! 
All the way to the bottom of the universe!” 
And no one told him 
that the universe has no bottom. 
Because the flamingo had pink feathers 
and a voice that couldn’t be interrupted.

“Lalalá!” he sang. 
“We’ll be upside down!” 
And indeed — he flew headfirst. 
His legs sticking up to the sky. 
His wings fluttering like scarves. 
And his eyes wide open.

“This is not flying,” the tick told him. 
“This is falling.” 
“That doesn’t matter!” the flamingo shouted. 
“As long as it has direction!”
And he kept falling. 

Stars, nebulae and dreams flickered around him, and beside him a clothesline rippled with a single sock — the one he couldn’t find in the morning.

“Where are you flying?” asked the mosquito, who was also clinging to his feathers. 
“I don’t know!” the flamingo laughed. 
“But it feels wonderful!”

And then — silence. 

Silence so deep 
that even the universe held its breath. 
The flamingo landed. 
Not on the ground. 
On the bottom. 
The bottom of the universe. 

Where no one was. 
Except him, 
the mosquito, 
and the tick he had.
And one mouse 
who was writing down 
what happened.

“Are you alright?” she asked. 
“I’m upside down,” said the flamingo. 
“But otherwise excellent.”

The mouse noted: 
“Flamingo — falls with grace. 
Even when he doesn’t know where. 
As long as he sings.”

And the flamingo smiled. 
An intoxicated mosquito fell out of his feathers. 
And from his pocket fell a map 
that pointed only downward.

“Next time I’ll fly even lower,” he said. 
“And what if lower isn’t possible?” 
“Then I’ll invent a new bottom.”

And he took off again. 
Headfirst. 
Heart upward. 
And he sang: 
“Lalaláááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááááá
THE END

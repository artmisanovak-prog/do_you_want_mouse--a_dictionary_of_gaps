# 🦚 CARD: PEACOCK – Sample

**Narcissistic distortion of reality**

This card diagnoses and addresses the problem of self‑centered thinking, where a team or individual reduces everything to their own schema, projects themselves onto others, and loses the ability to see the world objectively.

## 3 Diagnostic Questions (for this specific problem)

1. **Q1 (Interpretation):** “Why did the peacock see in every animal only a different version of himself? What does that reveal about his perception of reality?”

## Sample of 2 Angles out of 12

### 1. 👁️ ANGLE OF NARCISSISTIC DISTORTION
**Problem:** Pathological projection — inability to see others as independent beings  
- **Example in the team:** When designers assume users think like they do. When management believes “what works for me works for everyone.”  
- **Solution:** Introduce mandatory “role swapping” — each month spend a day in the role of a completely different stakeholder (customer, supplier, newcomer).

### 2. 🔢 ANGLE OF QUANTIFICATION OBSESSION
**Problem:** Counting as a defense against chaos  
- **Example in the team:** When we measure everything but forget to ask whether what we measure makes sense. Metrics become reality.  
- **Solution:** Each month remove one metric that no longer serves a purpose and is “just being counted.” Replace it with a qualitative description.

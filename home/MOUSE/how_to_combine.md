# How to combine cards

## Basic principle

Each card in the "Want a mouse?" system represents one diagnostic framework. By combining two or more cards, you create **new, more complex diagnostic tools** that correspond to the reality of team dysfunctions – which are rarely isolated.

## Process of combining

### Step 1: Identification of primary problems
1. Go through individual cards
2. Identify which dysfunctions occur in your team
3. Select 2-3 cards that best describe these dysfunctions

### Step 2: Creation of combined framework
1. Take **diagnostic questions** from both cards
2. Join them into **new, combined questions**
3. Select **most relevant angles** from each card
4. Create **new angles** that emerge from the combination

### Step 3: Design of intervention
1. Combine **workshop activities** from both cards
2. Adapt them for solving the combined problem
3. Create **new rituals or rules**

## Rules of effective combination

### 1. **Maximum 3 cards at once**
- More cards create too complex a framework
- Start with 2 cards, add a third only if necessary

### 2. **Look for synergy, not just sum**
- Good combination: Flamingo + Tomcat = "Enthusiastic jumping into speech"
- Weak combination: Flamingo + Peacock = Two different problems without connection

### 3. **Keep it simple**
- Even complex problems can be solved with simple interventions
- 1-2 combined angles can be more effective than all 24

## Examples of good combinations

### 🔥 Flamingo + Tomcat
- **Problem:** Fast, enthusiastic, but uncontrolled decision-making
- **Result:** "Quick agreement without reflection"

### 🦚 Peacock + Tiger
- **Problem:** Self-centered clinging to traditions
- **Result:** "Narcissistic conservatism"

### 🐱 Tomcat + Tiger
- **Problem:** Suppression of authenticity for the sake of "efficiency"
- **Result:** "Efficient tyranny"

## Tools for combining

### 1. **Combination table**
Create a table with cards on X and Y axis. In each cell, write what combination of problems emerges.

### 2. **Combination workshop**
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Participants: 3-5 team members
- Output: 1-2 combined frameworks for solving current problems

### 3. **Template for combined card**
You can create your own template for combined cards with these sections:
- Combination name
- Combined problem
- 3 combined questions
- 3-5 combined angles
- Workshop proposal

---

## Advanced techniques

### 1. **Cascading combination**
Combine already combined cards with additional cards.

### 2. **Thematic combinations**
Group cards by themes (communication, decision-making, culture).

### 3. **Situational combinations**
Create combinations for specific situations (crisis, innovation, change).

> Combining cards is a creative process. There are no wrong combinations, only more or less useful ones.

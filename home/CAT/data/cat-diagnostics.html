🐈‍⬛ Tomcat – diagnostic mode

The tyranny of premature closure in communication

🐁 Facilitator guide (mouse)

You are the mouse. You sit, listen, take notes. You don't judge who is right — you map how the team thinks.

Important before starting:

  • Tomcat is not a person. It is a dysfunction — a way the team behaves at a certain moment.
  • The animals are dysfunctions. The girl on the card is speech. The animal is the dysfunction. The text between them is the space for interpretation.
  • Angles are not personality types. The same person looks from a different angle on a different day. This is not about putting people in boxes.
  • There is no right or wrong angle. The goal is to have as many as possible — to expand the view.
  • You assign them — based on what you hear. You choose the angle intuitively, based on the core of the answer.
  • The notebook saves continuously — it survives closing the browser or refreshing the page.

The key to angles is always available — expand it below whenever you need it.

🔑 Key to angles (for assignment)
Three questions for the team (Tomcat):

Q1 – Meaning: What do you think is the main point or message of this story?

Q2 – Engagement: Which specific sentence, image, or moment engaged you the most and why?

Q3 – Application: If this story were a guide or advice, what would it advise you to do (or not do) in your work life?

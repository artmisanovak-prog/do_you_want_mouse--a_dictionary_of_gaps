🐈‍⬛ The Cat – diagnostic mode

The tyranny of premature closure in communication

🐁 Instructions for the facilitator (the Mouse)

You are the Mouse. You sit, listen, and write things down. You do not judge who is right — you map how the team thinks.

Important before starting:

  • The Cat is not a person. It is a dysfunction — a way the team behaves at a certain moment.
  • The animals are dysfunctions. The girl on the card is speech. The animal is the disruption. The text between them is space for interpretation.
  • Angles are not personality types. The same person can look from a different angle on a different day. This is not about labeling people.
  • There is no right or wrong angle. The goal is to have as many as possible — to expand perspective.
  • You assign the angles — based on what you hear. Choose intuitively, according to the core of the response.
  • The notebook saves continuously — it survives closing the browser and refreshing the page.

The key to the angles is always available — expand it below whenever needed.

📖 The Cat Story
🔑 Key to the angles (for assigning)
Three questions for the team (The Cat):

Q1 – Meaning: What do you think is the main point or message of this story?

Q2 – Impact: Which specific sentence, image, or moment struck you the most and why?

Q3 – Application: If this story were advice, what would it tell you to do (or not do) in your work life?

# ABOUT THE TOMCAT

How the tomcat ate two ladybugs and hiccupped dots for half an hour.

He ate two – not three, not one – two – exactly –14 dots and it took half an hour – 30 minutes – exactly.
It came as it went.
Bang bang.

Snap and gulp and munch – or something like that and finally – smack, smack – twice – exactly.

There were two ladybugs living, each had seven dots, when a tomcat saw them it was a scramble.
At least for them.

After 14 dots
in one moment
the earth collapsed.
At least for the tomcat and his insides.

But the mouse, always curious.
Since childhood she used to go
and where she could she used to ask,
where did the dots on the ladybugs come from?
What are they for?
Why don't they create proper camouflage instead?

And that exact math..
Who from those who come into contact with them counts so precisely?

Well and that day – suddenly – it happened
and the mouse had her answer.

The tomcat smacked
and then again
and right after that
it began.

Half an hour and it was recorded – exactly.
The tomcat hiccupped dots and finished sentences!
Sometimes whole sentences!!

Only that at the same time he was jumping into people's speech,
by finishing for them what they didn't finish.

He jumped into everyone's speech with the end of a sentence

but always by a little

sometimes by quite a lot

before the sentence was originally supposed to end

or – before the sentence was originally supposed to end.

And for the whole half hour nobody could then
no – finish anything.
It wasn't possible.

For everyone before,
they got to it,
the tomcat ended the sentence.
Immediately.

The tomcat laughed at it,
but hiccupped on.
And hiccupped until he finished.

Imagine that:
He finished a sentence and then finished hiccupping.

And people could again finish their sentences themselves.
Immediately.

Only, as it sometimes happens,
even though nobody knew what,
everyone knew
that something wasn't the same.

And what once isn't the same today,
once isn't the same never.

Only unfinished sentences are all the same.
Once, today and tomorrow.

Ladybugs have unfinished sentence ends on them.
Things that are unswallowed.

The tomcat didn't know that – and he swallowed two.
But swallowed ends he hiccupped back
and people learned
that not everything can be swallowed.
And that a dot is sometimes stronger than a whole sentence.

The tomcat ate his fill and the mouse and sentences remained whole.
Thank God for that.
The luck of two ladybugs returned.

But only the tomcat and the little mouse noticed it.
And neither of them said anything.

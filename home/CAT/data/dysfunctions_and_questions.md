# CARD: TOMCAT – COMMUNICATION PATHOLOGY

**Core work dysfunction:**
Tyrannical premature closure. A team member (or the entire team) has a compulsive need to finish thoughts, sentences, and considerations of others, thereby:

1.  Stifles creativity and deeper understanding.
2.  Prevents discovery of hidden aspects of the problem ("unswallowed sentence ends").
3.  Creates an illusion of efficiency ("we closed it quickly"), at the cost of quality and engagement.
4.  Leaves a feeling that "something isn't the same" — thus causing permanent damage to trust and openness in communication.

**Three sharp work questions (for the team):**

1.  **Q1 (Behavior analysis):** "Why did the tomcat jump into people's speech and finish sentences for them? What did he gain by this and what did he destroy?"
2.  **Q2 (Identification in team):** "When and who in our team most often plays the role of the 'tomcat' — that is, prematurely closes discussions, finishes thoughts for others, or pushes for quick decisions without diving into the problem?"
3.  **Q3 (Remedy):** "How can we create the '14 dots' rule — that is, a mechanism that forces us to let every important thought be fully 'hiccupped out' and prevents us from 'swallowing' it prematurely?"

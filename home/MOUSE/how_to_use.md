# How to use the "Do You Want a Mouse?" system

## For individuals

1. **Start with the Mouse** – Go to [MOUSE](../MOUSE/index.html) and work through the diagnostic tool.
2. **Read the fairy tale** and answer the 3 questions.
3. **Evaluate your answers** using the Key to angles.
4. **Discover your thinking style** and which cards might be useful for you.

## For teams

1. **Let each team member** go through the diagnostic with the Mouse.
2. **Create a collective map** of thinking styles in the team.
3. **Identify potential problems** – if the team skews heavily toward certain angles, this may signal dysfunctions.
4. **Choose the right card** to address the identified problem.

## For facilitators / consultants

1. **Use the Mouse** as an introductory diagnostic tool for clients.
2. **Recommend specific cards** based on the problems identified.
3. **Use the workshops** from the cards as structured interventions.
4. **Combine cards** to address complex problems.

## Tips for effective use

- **Read the fairy tales out loud** – it creates a stronger experience and better understanding.
- **Answer anonymously** – you will get more honest responses.
- **Don't rush** – give thoughts time to develop.
- **Discuss the differences** – different perspectives are a value, not a problem.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Do I have to start with the Mouse?**  
A: No, but it is recommended. The Mouse will help you understand how the system works.

**Q: How much time does it take?**  
A: Diagnostic with the Mouse: 15–30 minutes. Workshop from a card: 90 minutes.

**Q: Can I combine cards?**  
A: Yes, that is one of the main advantages of the system. See the [combination example](../MOUSE/how_to_combine.md)  
or: [inside the card](../MOUSE/inside_cat.md)

**Q: Is it suitable for small teams?**  
A: Yes, for teams from 3 people. It can also be used for individuals or large organisations.

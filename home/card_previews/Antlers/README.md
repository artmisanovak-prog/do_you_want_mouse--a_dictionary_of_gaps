## Core dysfunction

**Identity as archive of performances.** The team (or individual) is defined not by what they do or who they are, but by how they are documented, evaluated, and exhibited. Every "lightning" of attention (meeting, presentation, report, praise) adds a branch to the "antlers" of image, but no deeper experience, resonance ("thunder"), or real change ("rain") occurs.

Identity becomes a "living album" of empty gestures, smiles, and moments that "no one wants to remember anymore," but which the subject must carry. It is about emptied identity built on real-time feedback – a being that is heavy from light (data, images, attention) and light from meaning.

## 📘 Fairy tale

## ABOUT THE ANTLERS that collected lightnings

There once was a person. Or a deer. Or something between.
She had antlers. But not ordinary ones.
They were lightning-collecting antlers.
Not for weather.
For memory.

Every day a lightning struck her.
Not from sky.
From camera.
From click.
From "cheeeese!"

A thousand times.
Lightning.
But never thunder.
No rain.
Just light.
And smile.
And memory that never asks whether it's true.

They called her Cheeeese.
Like "cheese," but with an echo.
Because when she smiled, it was like when a word tries to be an image.
And when she didn't smile, it was still photographed.

Her antlers grew every time someone photographed her.
One branch for every memory.
One twist for every "cheeeese!"
And so she became a living album.
She walked through the world and carried on her head all the moments that no one wanted to remember anymore.

So she walked on and went on
with antlers like antennas.
Heavy from light.
Light from meaning.
And when someone met her, they said:
"That's the one with antlers of memory. The one who never gets wet. The one who smiles even when she doesn't know why."

Since then it is said that when someone photographs you and you smile,
perhaps a new antler is just beginning to bud.

And that "cheeeese" is not just a sound.
It is a trace of speech in image.
It is lightning without storm.
It is memory pretending to be presence.

## 🎯 3 diagnostic questions

1. **Q1 (Inventory of our antlers):** "What are in our team those 'lightning-collecting antlers'? Which processes, metrics, reports or rituals generate empty 'lightnings' (light, data, image) without subsequent 'thunder' (real impact, discussion, change) and 'rain' (refreshment, growth, release)?"

2. **Q2 (Recognizing 'Cheeeese'):** "Who or what in our system says 'Cheeeese' – that is, triggers the moment of performative smile, recording, image creation? Is it a manager with evaluation camera? Culture of constant reporting? Pressure on public successes?"

3. **Q3 (Seeking thunder and rain):** "We are 'heavy from light and light from meaning.' What would 'thunder' (deep, resonating response to our work) and 'rain' (nourishing, refreshing feedback or change) mean for us? How can we transform the collection of lightnings into something that has weight and meaning?"

## 🧠 Sample angles (3 of 12)

### 1. ANGLE OF LIGHTNING WITHOUT STORM (Action without consequence)

**How it manifests in team:**
Team is overwhelmed with activities, meetings, reports that consume energy (lightnings), but never lead to resonating change (thunder) or refreshing result (rain). Illusion of movement emerges without real impact. Burnout equation: Light + Smile – Impact – Renewal = Empty memory.

**What to do about it (sample solution):**
For every new task or meeting ask the question: **"What 'thunder' (impact) and what 'rain' (benefit for us) do we expect from this?"** If there are none, it's another lightning into the antlers.

---

### 2. ANGLE OF HEAVY FROM LIGHT, LIGHT FROM MEANING (Overwhelm without significance)

**How it manifests in team:**
Team produces tons of outputs, presentations, data, metrics (is heavy from light), but at the same time feels that its work lacks deeper meaning (is light from meaning). Quantity replaces quality. Members are tired from the mass of work, but empty from its substance.

**What to do about it (sample solution):**
Conduct regular **"density of meaning tests"**: For every larger task stop and answer in one sentence: "Why are we doing this and why is it important?" If impossible, it's just light without value.

---

### 3. ANGLE OF CARRIER OF FOREIGN MEMORIES (Inheritances we don't want)

**How it manifests in team:**
Team is forced to carry the weight of unfulfilled promises, outdated strategies, legacy systems, and forgotten leadership failures. It becomes a "living album" for moments and decisions that no one wants to solve anymore, but everyone depends on.

**What to do about it (sample solution):**
Introduce ritual of **"removing antlers"** – regular (e.g., quarterly) meeting where team symbolically gets rid of (archives, cancels, delegates) old, unnecessary commitments and processes that someone else loaded on them. What in our "antlers" no longer serves us?

## 🛠️ Workshop (structure)

### Workshop: "COLLECTING LIGHTNINGS OR LIVING IN STORM?" – 90 minutes

**Goal:** Reveal what in team generates empty "lightnings" (activities without impact), identify lost "thunder" (resonance) and "rain" (renewal), and begin transforming energy from building image (antlers) to creating meaningful change.

**Part 1: Photographing our antlers (30 min)**
- Introduction, reading the fairy tale
- Individual writing: **YELLOW CARD** (lightning – activity without impact), **BLUE CARD** (antler – part of our external image), **GRAY CARD** (cheese/cheeeese – how outside sees us vs. what is our hidden name)
- Creating "portrait of team in system" – outline of figure with antlers, where cards are stuck

**Part 2: Summoning the storm (40 min)**
- Group work **'Alchemy of lightning'** – how to transform selected "lightning" into activity with resonance (thunder) and renewal (rain)
- Group work **'Removing antlers'** – proposal of "transformation ritual" for selected antler according to hidden "cheese"

**Part 3: New growth (20 min)**
- Selection of one idea for transformation and one ritual to introduce
- Creation of **"Manifest of authentic storm"** – common agreement on new approach

## 📊 Diagnostic matrix

| Dominant angle | Diagnosis | Intervention ("Removal of antlers") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lightning without storm** | Team is overwhelmed with activities without real impact. | **"Week without lightnings":** Cancel all non-essential reports and meetings. Instead introduce one short "thunder brief". |
| **Heavy from light, light from meaning** | Team produces tons of outputs, but lacks meaning. | **"Hunt for meaning":** For main task everyone writes what meaning they see in it. Together find common line. |
| **Carrier of foreign memories** | Team is burdened with inheritance of old projects and decisions. | **"Ceremony of burning antlers":** List all "inheritances" and symbolically/actually get rid of them. |
| **Growth from outside** | Team identity is defined by external evaluation. | **"Inner growth day":** Team evaluates itself according to own, internally set criteria. |
| **Memory as presence** | Team lives in past (old successes, procedures). | **"Day of amnesia":** One day solve problems as if they were completely new, regardless of "how it was done". |

## 💡 Why Antlers are key?

This is the card of digital age and performative culture. It perfectly continues **Crocodile** (commodification) and **Snake** (twisted language), but pushes them into era of total documentation and image. While Crocodile sells emotions and Snake has torn tongue, **Antlers** show how we become an archive to ourselves, a living presentation.

It is about teams driven by data from productivity tracking tools, about culture of "let's do it for LinkedIn," about impossibility of being invisible. It is the card of burnout from one's own image. Antlers add the crucial dimension of time and memory in digital form: **We are what is recorded about us.** And that is terrifyingly strong metaphor.

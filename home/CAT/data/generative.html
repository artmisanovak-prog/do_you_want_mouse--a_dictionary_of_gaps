🔮 Cat – generative mode

The Cat with itself

Did you know that this card can generate new workshops without needing another card?
Just combine two different angles. Here are 4 ready-made combinations — use them straight away.

🧩 Ready-made combinations

Combination 1 + 9 Angle 1 (The Physical End of a Sentence) × Angle 9 (The Unfinished as a Gift)
Full stop, or three dots?
Goal: Practise the shift from closing to opening.

Exercise (20 minutes):

  1. Take a current problem that "should have been decided long ago".
  2. 5 minutes: Every proposal must end with a full stop. No "maybe", no "what if". Only hard, finished decisions.
  3. 5 minutes: Every proposal must end with three dots. No closure, only opening, questions, possibilities.
  4. 10 minutes: Reflection – When does a full stop help us? When do three dots help us? Where in our work do we need more of one or the other?
Combination 2 + 6 Angle 2 (Quasi-efficiency) × Angle 6 (The Tyranny of Metrics)
14 full stops we don't see
Goal: Reveal what numbers cannot capture.

Exercise (25 minutes):

  1. Each team member writes one metric the team regularly tracks on a slip of paper.
  2. The slips are shuffled and each person draws one from someone else.
  3. Task: "Invent the story this metric does NOT tell. What is happening behind the scenes? Who or what is suffering? What successes remain invisible?"
  4. Share. Output: a list of "14 full stops" – things we should start tracking but have not measured yet.
Combination 4 + 7 Angle 4 (The Observing Mouse) × Angle 7 (Forced Consensus)
The silent witness speaks
Goal: Give voice to the quiet members and reveal false consensus.

Exercise (30 minutes):

  1. Appoint one person as the "Mouse" — they only observe, take no active part.
  2. Hold a normal discussion on a current topic (15 minutes).
  3. The Mouse then shares observations: Who spoke most? Who was interrupted? Were there moments of silence that felt like forced agreement?
  4. Reflection: How does it change the meeting when someone is watching without judging?
Combination 5 + 10 Angle 5 (Lasting Change) × Angle 10 (Communication Trauma)
Rebuilding after the cat
Goal: Map the damage and start restoring trust.

Exercise (35 minutes):

  1. Anonymous written round: "Describe one moment when you felt your voice was swallowed in this team."
  2. Read the responses aloud (without attribution). The group listens — no reactions, no justifications.
  3. Together identify: what patterns repeat? What do people most fear?
  4. Create one concrete rule that would prevent the most frequently mentioned situation.

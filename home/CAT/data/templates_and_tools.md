# TEMPLATES AND TOOLS

## Specific ideas and tools from 12 work angles:

### From Angle 3 (Unswallowable content):
*   **Wall of unswallowed:** Physical or virtual place (Flipchart, Miro board, shared document) where anyone can anonymously write down an "unswallowed sentence end" from discussion. Regularly (e.g., at the start of weekly meeting) points are reviewed and added to agenda.

### From Angle 10 (Communication trauma):
*   **Safety audit:** Anonymous questionnaire with questions like:
    *   Do I feel safe expressing disagreement?
    *   Am I often interrupted?
    *   Are my ideas and concerns taken seriously?
    *   Do I feel I can open a difficult topic without fear of ridicule?
    *   (Results serve as basis for setting new rules.)

### From Angle 12 (Returned luck):
*   **Ladybug ritual:** Establish a silent symbol (e.g., small toy ladybug, card with image) that anyone who feels "interrupted" or "swallowed" can place before them on the table. It is a signal for others to slow down, give space, and actively try to listen to the person.

### From Angle 8 (Competition instead of collaboration):
*   **Talking stick (speaking object/stick):** Rule that during key discussions, only the one holding a designated object (pen, toy, special stone) speaks. Others actively listen. The object is passed around.

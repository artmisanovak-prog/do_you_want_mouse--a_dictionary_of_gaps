# DIAGNOSTIC MATRIX – TOMCAT

## How to recognize the prevailing angle from the answer?

| Prevailing angle | Team diagnosis | Immediate intervention |
| ---------------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| **1. Tyranny of the dot** | Team believes that quick closure = efficiency. Discussions are cut before they begin. | Introduce **Rule of three dots**. The first one to say "dot" only summarizes, does not close. |
| **2. Quasi-efficiency** | Team measures success by meeting length, not output quality. Speed is valued higher than understanding. | Measure meeting success by **number of open questions at the end**. The more, the better. |
| **3. Unswallowable content** | Team avoids difficult topics. "Elephants in the room" are not addressed, atmosphere is suffocating. | Create **Wall of unswallowed**. Anonymous place for things that weren't said. |
| **4. Observant mouse** | Silent voices exist in the team that no one hears. Introverts have no space. | Appoint **Mouse** for each discussion. Just observes, at the end shares who spoke and who was interrupted. |
| **5. Permanent change** | Team experienced communication trauma. Even when the tomcat is silent, people are afraid to speak. | Leader must **intentionally create unfinished sentences** and invite others to finish them. |
| **6. Tyranny of metrics** | Team only solves numbers (done/not done), not stories behind them. | Add **qualitative story** to every metric. "What 14 dots did we hiccup?" |
| **7. Violent consensus** | Silence is interpreted as agreement. People have resigned. | Introduce **silence of consent = disagreement**. Whoever is silent automatically disagrees. |
| **8. Competition instead of collaboration** | Interrupting is a race for dominance. Whoever finishes the sentence first, wins. | Introduce **talking object**. Only the one holding it speaks. |
| **9. Unfinished as gift** | Team jumps straight to solutions, cannot stay with the problem. | Exercise **"Our problem is like..."** Forbid finishing. Only beginnings of metaphors. |
| **10. Communication trauma** | Meetings are a source of stress. People fear humiliation. | Conduct **audit of communication safety**. Anonymous questionnaire. |
| **11. Strong dot** | Formal closure ("So decided!") carries more weight than discussion content. | When closing, ask: **"Who feels their voice wasn't heard?"** |
| **12. Returned luck** | Team is slowly healing, but change is fragile. | Introduce **Ladybug ritual**. Whoever is interrupted, places a ladybug on the table. |

## 3 Diagnostic Questions (for this specific problem)

1. **Q1 (Interpretation):** “Why does the flamingo prefer to sing ‘Lalalá’ and declare ‘As long as it has direction!’ instead of admitting that he is falling?”
2. **Q2 (Identification):** “Where in your team/project do you observe ‘singing during the fall’ — celebrating energy, activity, and progress that hides the absence of a real goal?”
3. **Q3 (Transformation):** “What would need to happen for your team to gain the ‘mouse perspective’ — the ability to calmly observe reality and ask simple, essential questions?”

---

## Sample of 2 Angles out of 12

### 1. 🎭 ANGLE OF TOXIC OPTIMISM
**Problem:** Enthusiasm as a defense against reality  
- **Example in the team:** When the team responds to every warning or bad news with even more enthusiasm and positive rhetoric.  
- **Solution:** Create a safe space for “bulletproof reality.” Introduce a ritual where the first word in every brainstorming session must be critical/realistic (“mouse view”), and only then can energy come in (“flamingo song”).

### 2. 🏃 ANGLE OF PERFORMATIVE ENERGY
**Problem:** Action as a goal in itself  
- **Example in the team:** When the team measures success by activity (number of meetings, hours worked) instead of results and impact.  
- **Solution:** Reevaluate metrics: Celebrate only results that have impact, not activity itself. Ask: “If we removed this movement/activity, would we lose anything valuable?”

# 🔑 Key to angles – Diagnostic table

This key serves to analyze natural cognitive preferences based on answers to 3 universal questions.

## 3 universal questions (for any fairy tale)

1. **Q1 (Meaning):** "What do you think is the main point or message of this story?"
2. **Q2 (Engagement):** "What specifically from your environment does this remind you of and WHY?"
3. **Q3 (Application):** "If this story were a guide or advice, what would it advise you to do (or not do) in your work/project life?"

## 12 universal angles (sample of first 3)

### 1. 🌀 ABSURDIST ANGLE (Logic upside down)
**Core:** Sees the contradiction between expected logic and absurd reality.
- **Q1 answer:** "That the world doesn't make sense and trying to make it logical is futile."
- **Q2 example:** "What engaged me most was that the tomcat hiccups dots, because it's perfect nonsense."
- **Q3 advice:** "Would advise not to do things just because 'that's how it's done', if it doesn't make sense."

### 2. 💬 LINGUISTIC ANGLE (Play with words)
**Core:** Focuses on form, sound, and structure of language.
- **Q1 answer:** "The point is in the question 'Want a mouse?'. It's a linguistic trap."
- **Q2 example:** "I was struck by the sentence 'hiccupped dots for half an hour' – that rhythm and concreteness."
- **Q3 advice:** "Would advise paying attention to which word we choose first."

### 3. 😏 IRONIC ANGLE (Twisting into the ridiculous)
**Core:** Sees the gap between intention and result.
- **Q1 answer:** "That what is supposed to be a solution creates a completely different problem."
- **Q2 example:** "What amused me most was that the peacock counts his eyespots when no one is watching."
- **Q3 advice:** "Would advise asking: 'And what if it turns out exactly the opposite?'"

*Remaining 9 angles are part of the full diagnostic set.*

---

## How to use

1. Gather anonymous answers from team members to the 3 questions.
2. For each answer, find the most fitting angle.
3. Count occurrences of each angle.
4. You get a "cognitive map of the team".

> Complete diagnostic table with 12 angles is part of the full version of the system.
